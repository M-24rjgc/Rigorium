# Design Research

Use `research_design` to materialize a decision package and `research_brief` to revise its brief.

- Choose `discover` for a broad domain and `complete` for a user-provided idea.
- Develop at least two genuinely different mechanisms. Give each a theory or algorithm claim, falsification condition, stop rule, baselines, evaluation protocol, compute bound, and ethics treatment.
- Link evidence, run similar-work and evidence rescans, and include independent criticism before comparison.
- Score every candidate against the same weighted objectives. Preserve the Pareto frontier and reversible elimination reasons.
- Keep contradictions and unresolved risks visible. Require explicit confirmation before selecting through unresolved high-severity findings.
- Treat titles as mutable metadata. Confirm an exact final title only after explicit user approval; revisions must supersede the previous brief.
